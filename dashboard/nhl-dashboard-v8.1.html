HOW TO READ THESE PREDICTIONS
PREDICTED SCORE — The model's expected final score based on each team's offensive output, goalie performance, power play/penalty kill efficiency, home ice advantage, and recent form (last 10 games).
WIN PROBABILITY % — Calculated exactly (no random simulation) from a Poisson distribution. The highlighted team abbreviation is the predicted winner. Percentages closer to 50/50 indicate an evenly matched game.
HIGH CONFIDENCE — Win probability spread >35%. The model strongly favours one team. MEDIUM = 20–35% spread. LOW = under 20% — treat as a toss-up.
GOALIE SV% — A higher save percentage means the goalie is expected to stop more shots. This is one of the model's most impactful factors — a hot goalie can significantly shift win probability.
⚡ B2B — Back-to-back game. This team played yesterday. The model applies a fatigue penalty to their expected goal output, which is reflected in the predicted score and win probability.
INJURY ADJUSTED — If shown, the predictions have been manually adjusted to account for a key player injury. The model applies a goal-output penalty to that team's λ (lambda) scoring rate.
